1a. Legal Bases for Processing

Where we process personal data, processing is based on the following legal bases:

  • Art. 6 (1) lit. a GDPR consent. This applies exclusively to: error and usage statistics (section 6.1) and the submission of feedback (section 6.5). Both are disabled by default or purely voluntary and can be withdrawn at any time.
  • Art. 6 (1) lit. b GDPR performance of a contract: provision of App functions, map display, weather retrieval, unlocking of purchased features.
  • Art. 6 (1) lit. c GDPR legal obligation (e.g. retention duties under commercial / tax law).
  • Art. 6 (1) lit. f GDPR legitimate interest: prevention of abuse and ensuring technical operation.

No breach of the prohibition of bundling: Accepting the EULA, Terms and this Privacy Policy on first launch constitutes acknowledgement of the contractual terms and is not a data protection consent. Processing that requires consent is requested separately, voluntarily and revocably (Art. 7 (4) GDPR).

Legal source: The General Data Protection Regulation is available at eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj (Regulation (EU) 2016/679).

6. Recipients and Third-Party Providers

The following lists every party to which personal data may be transmitted while using the App. A transfer only takes place in the case described in each instance.

6.1 Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics (Google Ireland Limited)

  • Purpose: Crash reports and aggregated usage statistics for bug fixing and improving the App.
  • Data: Device model, OS version, app version, crash stack trace, randomly generated installation ID, timestamp, coarse region.
  • Legal basis: Consent, Art. 6 (1) lit. a GDPR.
  • Disabled by default. Collection only begins once you enable it in the app settings under Error and usage statistics. You may switch it off again at any time; collection then stops immediately. The App can be used in full without this consent.
  • Privacy information: firebase.google.com/support/privacy

6.2 Google Play Billing and Google Play Core (Google Commerce Limited / Google Ireland Limited)

  • Purpose: Processing of purchases and subscriptions, verification of purchase status, and delivery of app updates.
  • Data: Google account identifier, purchase token, product ID, app and device details. The Provider receives no payment data.
  • Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) lit. b GDPR (performance of a contract / provision of purchased features).
  • Note: The seller of the premium features is Google Commerce Limited, not the Provider. See Terms 9.

6.3 Map display: MapLibre and CARTO Basemaps

  • Purpose: Displaying the locations of your hives on a map.
  • Data: When map tiles are loaded, your IP address is transmitted to the map service CARTO (basemaps.cartocdn.com), together with the requested map section. Map data originates from OpenStreetMap contributors.
  • Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) lit. b GDPR (provision of the map feature).
  • Avoidability: The transfer only occurs when you open a map view. The MapLibre Native library itself does not transmit any usage or telemetry data.

6.4 Weather data

Depending on the location, the following providers are used to display weather data and derive treatment recommendations (e.g. suitability for varroa treatment):

  • Bright Sky (private open-source project; data source: German Weather Service) for Germany and Bulgaria (CC BY 4.0). The geographic coordinates of the queried location and the IP address are transmitted to api.brightsky.dev. No account data or identifiers are sent.
  • National weather services for the Czech Republic (CHMI/ČHMÚ), Hungary (HungaroMet), Poland (IMGW-PIB) and Slovakia (SHMÚ) (respectively CC BY 4.0 / CC BY-SA). These access public station data; no user location coordinates are transmitted to the services. Only the devices IP address is technically transmitted.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1)(b) GDPR.

Note: With Bright Sky, the coordinates may allow conclusions to be drawn about the location of your apiaries. The query is only performed if coordinates are stored for a location.

6.5 Feedback function (Supabase, EU region Ireland)

  • Purpose: Transmission of voluntary user feedback.
  • Data: Category, rating, free-text comment, app version and a pseudonymous device identifier. The latter is formed as a SHA-256 hash of the Android device ID and serves solely to limit submissions to one per device and period. It does not allow the Provider to identify you.
  • Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) lit. a GDPR (voluntary transmission by actively submitting the form).
  • Processing on our behalf: Supabase Inc., hosting in the EU (Ireland), agreement pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR, standard contractual clauses.

6.6 Account management and AI credit balance (credits)

For AI features (in particular the voice assistant), the user can create an account and purchase credits (AI credit balance). The following data is processed:

  • Account data: Email address and password hash for login as well as the Supabase user ID. Processing is performed via Supabase Inc. (EU, Ireland), legal basis Art. 6 (1) lit. b GDPR (performance of a contract).
  • Credit balance and usage: The current credit balance and the usage per AI request are stored server-side (evidence of service provision, Art. 6 (1) lit. b GDPR).
  • Aggregated usage statistics: To ensure operation and for capacity planning, aggregated metrics that cannot be related to individuals (e.g. number of AI interactions and average conversation length) are collected on the basis of our legitimate interest (Art. 6 (1) lit. f GDPR).
  • Consent records: The consents granted when using the account are stored pursuant to section 6.8.

The credit balance does not expire. If the account is deleted, any remaining credit balance is refunded upon request; the associated data is deleted together with the account (account deletion, Art. 17 GDPR).

6.7 Device-side operating system services

  • Speech recognition / speech output: If you use dictation or read-aloud features, the service configured on your device (depending on the manufacturer, e.g. Google or Samsung) processes the voice input. That service is subject to the privacy policy of the respective provider; the Provider of this App does not receive the audio data.
  • Location services: Location is determined via your device's services and only after you have expressly granted permission.

6.8 Consent records (Art. 7 (1) GDPR)

For the purpose of proving that consent has been given (the controller's burden of proof under Art. 7 (1) GDPR), a record of consent is stored when you accept the EULA, the Terms and this Privacy Policy. This occurs as soon as an account is created or used. The record is stored encrypted at Supabase (EU, Ireland) and contains:

  • User identifier (Supabase user ID) and email address
  • Version of the accepted policy and the time of consent
  • SHA-256 checksum (hash) of the policy text in force at the time of consent
  • User agent (app version/device) and the IP address detected by the server
  • Type of consent (e.g. PRIVACY, EULA, TERMS) and legal jurisdiction

Retention: Without premium purchases, the record is deleted when the account is deleted. With premium purchases, the record is kept for the duration of use and thereafter only for as long as is necessary to defend against potential claims (at the latest until the expiry of the limitation period, 3 years pursuant to § 195 BGB); in the event of legal disputes, deletion is deferred until the end of the proceedings plus 3 years (legal hold).

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) lit. f GDPR (legitimate interest in evidencing consent or defending against legal claims).

6.10 AI voice assistant Microsoft Azure AI Speech

For the optional AI voice assistant (premium feature), voice input (speech recognition, STT) and voice output (text-to-speech, TTS) are processed via the Azure AI Speech service from Microsoft. The following applies:

  • Purpose: Converting your voice input into text (STT) for interacting with the voice assistant, and outputting the answer as speech (TTS).
  • Company: Microsoft Corporation (Microsoft Ireland Operations Ltd. as EU provider).
  • Data: Audio data (voice recording) and the technical data required for processing. Transmission is encrypted (TLS). The Azure subscription key is stored solely server-side; the App only sends a short-lived, JWT-protected access token.
  • Storage location: EU data centre (region Germany West Central, germanywestcentral). Azure AI Speech processes data only in the region of the resource location.
  • Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) lit. b GDPR (performance of a contract, provision of the purchased premium feature) or consent, insofar as on-device speech recognition is not sufficient.
  • Processing on our behalf: There is a data processing agreement (DPA) with Microsoft pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR within the framework of the Microsoft Online Services Terms and the Microsoft Data Protection Addendum. Microsoft does not use the data to improve its own AI models.
  • Privacy notices: microsoft.com/privacy and Data Protection Addendum.

6.12 Google OAuth (Google Ireland Limited)

For signing in with your Google account ("Sign in with Google"), Google OAuth 2.0 is offered as a sign-in method. The following data is processed:

  • Purpose: Authentication and account creation when signing in with your Google account.
  • Data: Google account email address, Google profile name, Google profile picture (optional) and the Google user ID. The app requests only the openid, email and profile scopes – no access to Gmail, Google Drive or other Google services.
  • Company: Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (for users in the EU).
  • Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) lit. b GDPR (performance of a contract – provision of the chosen sign-in method).
  • Transfer to Supabase: The authentication data is processed via Supabase Inc. (EU, Ireland) as data processor (JWT management, accounts), contract pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR, EU standard contractual clauses.

6.13 Cloud backup (optional premium feature, Scaleway Object Storage)

For premium users there is the optional possibility to create an encrypted cloud backup of your hive data (backup in the event of device loss or device change). The following applies:

  • Purpose: Encrypted backup of your local hive data (hives, colonies, inspections, photos, finances) in the cloud so that you can restore it after a device loss or device change.
  • Encryption (E2E): The backup is encrypted client-side with a passphrase of your choice (PBKDF2 → AES-256-GCM) before it is uploaded. The providers cannot read the contents. The passphrase is not stored on servers; if you lose it, restoration is not possible (no recovery path).
  • Company (storage): Scaleway S.A.S. (France) – Scaleway Object Storage (S3-compatible), EU data centres. The encrypted containers are stored there.
  • Control (Supabase): The management (which device is allowed to upload, backup metadata) is handled via Supabase Inc. (EU, Ireland) as data processor. Supabase receives only metadata (timestamp, size, checksum) – not the contents.
  • Master device model: Exactly one device ("Master") may upload backups; other devices can only restore backups. You can change the master device at any time.
  • Automatic backups: After activation, the app automatically creates a backup about every two weeks (only with a network connection), provided the passphrase is stored on the device.
  • Retention: Backups are retained for a maximum of 9 months and then automatically deleted.
  • Consent: Storage takes place only after your express consent (Art. 6 (1) lit. a GDPR). You can withdraw the consent at any time – all cloud backups will then be deleted (Art. 17 GDPR).
  • Account deletion: When you delete your account, all cloud backups are also deleted.

6.14 Mistral AI (AI language model, OCR and speech processing)

For AI features the Provider uses services of Mistral AI SAS, 15 rue des Halles, 75001 Paris, France (EU):

  • Purpose: AI assistant answers, knowledge search (embeddings), text recognition of scanned receipts (OCR), speech recognition (STT) and speech output (TTS).
  • Data: Text content and prompts, voice recordings and transcripts, technical metadata. Transmission is encrypted (TLS); the API key is held exclusively server-side.
  • No training: Use of API calls to train the Mistral models is disabled. The App does not offer a feedback feature (thumbs up/down).
  • Retention: Inputs and outputs are stored for the duration of processing and for up to 30 days for abuse monitoring (zero data retention is not agreed).
  • Location and processing: EU (France); DPA pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR; sub-processors: trust.mistral.ai/subprocessors.
  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (provision of the purchased premium feature) or consent.
  • Privacy notice: legal.mistral.ai/terms/privacy-policy.

6.15 Cloudflare Turnstile (CAPTCHA at sign-in)

To protect against automated sign-ins, Cloudflare Turnstile may be used during account sign-in:

  • Purpose: Distinguishing human from automated access (CAPTCHA) during sign-in and registration.
  • Data: IP address, browser and device information, and interaction data; the widget is loaded from challenges.cloudflare.com in a WebView. According to the provider, Turnstile does not set advertising cookies.
  • Company: Cloudflare, Inc., 101 Townsend St., San Francisco, CA 94107, USA (Cloudflare Germany GmbH as EU representative).
  • Processing: DPA pursuant to Art. 28 GDPR; transfer to the USA based on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (Art. 46 GDPR).
  • Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (security of sign-in, abuse prevention) or (b) where necessary for sign-in.
  • Privacy notice: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.
